# EnerMAKER Lab

Academic homepage of Prof. Jin-Kyeom Kim, Department of Marine New Materials Convergence Engineering, Korea Maritime & Ocean University (KMOU).

Research focuses on triboelectric energy harvesting, self-powered sensing and bioelectronics, functional nanomaterials and interfaces, and computational materials/device design.

- Principal Investigator: Jin-Kyeom Kim
- Laboratory: Advanced Energy Materials and Functional Devices Laboratory (EnerMAKER Lab)
